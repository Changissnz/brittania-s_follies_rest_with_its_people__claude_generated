# Brittania's Follies Rest with Its People

Some elaboration on fictional characters from Brittania and Germania, further adding evidence to Brittania's undeniable decline during the rule of American President Crumbs. Generated by Claude Sonnet 4.6.

Brittania was not in a good place during the second quarter of the 21st century, as mentioned through stories such as [A fictionalized story about a rising star, of Hispania, in the American LEO community](https://github.com/Changissnz/changes_in_law_and_order_in_america__claude_generated), [A story briefing Officer Smith's ending years as an LEO](https://github.com/Changissnz/officer_smith-s_ending_years_at_the_DOJ__claude_generated), [A short story about Ashtabanos Morichito, during the years of Hispania's rise in the United States](https://github.com/Changissnz/a_little_story_on_hispania-s_meteoric_rise__claude_generated), and [A fictionalized account of President Crumbs' American rule during the time of Hispania's meteoric rise](https://github.com/Changissnz/president_crumbs_helped_hispania_by_saving_brittania_from_murder__claude_generated). Hispania did not simply siphon power from Brittania. But in terms of where the power went, due to the vacuum left behind by Brittania — especially due to its far-right power struggles — Hispania indubitably takes the crown. This is metaphorical of course, since Brittania still had a king in that era.

In the thirteenth chapter of [Twenty five fictional tales about human organization gone wrong](https://github.com/Changissnz/the_charity_of_hate#Kourtney-Kooneys-Tactic), the character of Kourtney Kooney was explored. Her tactic: give the man tacos. While he is eating those tacos, sneak up behind him and stab him in the back with a knife.

Kourtney Kooney grew up a sweet, kind, and considerate girl. All her classmates in grade school said she was quite helpful, open, and had a great sense of humor, even when that humor was somewhat mean. The conditioning that turned that positivity into what she became — an assassin, practically — was not documented cleanly. The strains from the American-Schinian trade wars was one of the critical points that produced characters such as Kourtney Kooney.

The women of Brittania were not known for being murderesses. Brittania, due to its relatively long tradition of Victorianism, was not inclined to let its women delve into the physical martial arts. That is a stereotype on Brittanian female delicacy. There were plenty of Brittanian females quite strong and athletic, able to pummel typical Schinian males — especially the ones that did nothing but knit sweaters in factories all day — into the ground with little effort.

So judging by Kourtney Kooney's occupation, which she loved so much it might as well have been a pastime, the culture of Brittania was undergoing a shift, a de-civilization per se. Her winning streak by use of the taco tactic was cut short, resulting in her death, when she tried it on Mariannas Isaacson, whose sexuality sent out strange signals that disrupted Kourtney's trained choreography. The tactic did not work. But the results from her occupation cut short did. The word was out. Brittania was not exactly shy on permitting secret agents such as Kourtney to embark on tasks for the greatness of Brittania.

Jhennah Sutterson, explored in [A story about how one of America's Southern darlings fared during the golden years and how she had to be protected, during these dire socioeconomic times](https://github.com/Changissnz/jhennah_sutterson_another_southern_darling__claude_generated), [A short story about Ashtabanos Morichito, during the years of Hispania's rise in the United States](https://github.com/Changissnz/a_little_story_on_hispania-s_meteoric_rise__claude_generated), and [A description of two fictional female characters that exemplifies the axial transition of Western power from Brittania to Hispania](https://github.com/Changissnz/more_on_the_societal_issues_under_the_crumbs_administration__claude_generated), was quite the Brittanian elite in Austin, Texas, due to her marriage to a Welsh royal. Her days spent as a queen of sorts in Austin were not a pony show every day. Times were getting tough. Mughalia and India's growing apathy to the commercial concerns of Brittania had impacted her family's corporate welfare.

On one occasion, Jhennah was sitting down at the dinner table, eating leftovers with her father-in-law, Robert. Robert's wife had died quite some time ago, and he had not dated anyone ever since. Robert started by making awkward eye contact with Jhennah. Jhennah, the Southern darling that she was, tried keeping her head down multiple times. Robert then made his advances, sexual in nature. He exclaimed how she reminded him of his high school crush — her hair the same color, her polite Victorian demeanor virtually identical. Jhennah attempted to deflect his propositions by simply saying "thanks" and continuing eating. Robert, although an old man, suddenly leaped across the table, tackling Jhennah to the ground. What came next was intercourse, to succinctly put it.

After that event, Jhennah did not dare mention anything of it to her husband William. On one occasion, William asked her about Robert. Jhennah started a light laughing and weeping that turned into an outright sobbing and screaming. William was somewhat of a chauvinist. He immediately told her she was being hysterical: "if there are any specific concerns, out with it". Jhennah told him. He told her she was mentally ill, and tied her with rope to the dinner table. William, at that point in time, had developed a habit of physically tying his wife to the dinner table when she upset him.

Sigyn Thornprikkensen, a main character in [A short story about the minor character, Sigyn Thornprikkensen, from some of my other LLM-generated stories](https://github.com/Changissnz/sigyn_thornprikkensen_died_fast_in_a_declining_america__grok_generated) and a minor character in [A novel generated by Claude Sonnet 4.5](https://github.com/Changissnz/such_utter_romantics_of_florida__of_Fiction_and_fantasia), was of Germania, unlike the previous two females. Her character is important in noting the dissolving unity of Europe, especially since the Schlavian Civil War.

During the last months of her life, her relationship with the head honcho, Emiliano, had been severed. She was single and ready to mingle. Sigyn had this way of drawing in men. She maintained an emotional distance to them, while being friendly. She knew how and when to smile. When she smiled, she would do so by pulling in her upper lip, thereby making her lower lip appear twice the width of her upper lip — her upper and lower lips were roughly of the same width. She practiced smiling that way because it gave her a more Brittanian appearance. During this time period, despite the decline of Brittania in conjunction with the meteoric rise of Hispania, Brittania still had prestige. So it was socially advantageous for women from all ethno-backgrounds, especially Germania — since Brittania and Germania have perhaps the longest history out of the four ethno-realms: Brittania, Gaullia, Germania, and Hispania — to appear and act in the manner of Brittania.

During the last months of her life, Sigyn would invite men over. Her openness for those kinds of acts came very easily, especially since her encounter with Bolsonaro's affiliates. On practically every occasion, she would initiate the contact and entry by saying this exact phrase: "Tylerios would be furious." There was the psychology of the forbidden apple behind this. Similar to a woman telling a man "you cannot have this" and then entirely stripping off her clothes in front of him.

If she had served in the inner circles of the Brittanian courts, she surely would not have done those things. That was how some members of the Germanian populace chose to establish and maintain relations. They put themselves at risk, advancing instead of retreating like Brittania, and were not as keen on maintaining lines of segregation in the manner of Brittania — a strategy that further removed Brittania from many important tables for discussion.
